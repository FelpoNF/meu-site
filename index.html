What creators ask first.

Do I have to write anything?
No. We work from content you've already published. You read drafts and tell us what feels wrong. Nobody asks you for a document, a file, or a login.
Will it actually sound like me?
That's the only thing we're selling. We study how you speak, the phrases you repeat, the way you explain things. Nothing releases until you say it sounds like you.
What if I don't like the first draft?
You send it back with notes and we rewrite it. There is no version of this where a product goes out that you didn't approve first.
What happens after it's live?
We deliver the files and the announcement copy. You post it once, in your own voice. Everything between the buyer and the product is ours to handle.
What if now isn't the right moment?
Then say so. We'll come back when the timing works for you. Nothing is signed until you sign it.
