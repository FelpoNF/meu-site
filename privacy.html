Byline Studio

Almost nothing here is collected.

This page exists so you can check that claim in under two minutes.

This website

No accounts, no forms, no tracking pixels, no advertising cookies, no analytics script. Nothing is written to your device by this site.

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives the usual request information (IP address, browser) when your device fetches them. That is the only third party involved in viewing this page.

If you email us

We receive your address, your name if you sign it, and whatever you write. We use it to answer you and to talk about the project. We don't add you to a newsletter for it, and we don't pass it to anyone else.

If you book a call

The booking tool is a separate service with its own privacy notice, shown before you confirm a time. It gives us your name, email and the slot you chose. We use it to show up at the right hour.

If we work together

Anything you send us as a partner — drafts, recordings, audience data, account access — is covered by the agreement we sign, which is stricter than this page. That document controls.

Deleting it

Write to studiobyline@gmail.com and ask. We delete what we hold about you, except records we're legally required to keep, and we confirm by reply.
